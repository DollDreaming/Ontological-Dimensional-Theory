# Open problems

**What remains is the work of setting the formulas up from structural declarations into calculable formulas, and the questions the author has not yet settled.**

## Where the calculation is not yet settled <span class="st st-rev">Open</span> { #formalization data-toc-label="Where the calculation is not yet settled" }

The main paper gives the form of a formula for all nine dimensions. In some places only the name and character of an operator are given, and how to calculate it is not yet decided. The author holds that the formulas are now at a stage of going beyond structural declarations (2026-09-24).

| Place | What the main paper gives | What is open |
|---|---|---|
| Generation between dimensions $\bigoplus$, $\Phi$ | Phase overlap of the lower dimensions, "not a simple sum but a topological accumulation" | How to calculate the overlap |
| Arrangement rule $A$ of the 4th dimension | "Not a simple sum but particular patterns and rules" | The form of those patterns and rules |
| 7th dimension | Center line and residual traces; averaging and integration are one expression | A form that holds both the center line and the residual traces |
| Reflection–projection operator $R$ of the 8th dimension | The interaction of the 7th dimension with a stimulus | How to calculate reflection and projection |
| Synthesis operator $\Omega$ of the 9th dimension | Processing, interpretation, compression | The form of the compression |

→ [Core principles](../principles/index.md#topological-accumulation) · [The nine dimensions](../dimensions/index.md)

## What the author has not yet decided <span class="st st-rev">Open</span> { #undecided data-toc-label="What the author has not yet decided" }

- Whether each dimension uses a different operation or they continue as one function cannot yet be confirmed; the author allows that it may be a special case (2026-09-20).
- Whether there must be exactly nine dimensions is not confirmed. Not confirmed does not mean wrong (held open by the author, 2026-09-20).
- Whether to treat the 7th dimension as the place where 'establishment' happens is not decided (2026-09-21).
- The author asks whether the amount of change when a dimension rises is right, and whether two fractal patterns are being combined.

> "A point changes into a line, a line into a plane — is this amount of change right?" — Author, 2026-09-20[^q1]

> "Could it be that two fractal patterns are being combined?" — Author, 2026-09-22[^q2]

- What is energy?

> "Temperature is energy, and when the inherent energy increases, interactions inevitably increase. Then there is something I ultimately want to ask: what, after all, is energy?" — Author, 2026-09-24[^q3]

## Where the theory could be wrong <span class="st st-hyp">Hypothesis</span> { #falsification data-toc-label="Where the theory could be wrong" }

The author himself states that the theory's largest claim is also the point where it could be wrong.

> "There is nothing that the nine-dimensional fractal structure cannot explain. — This is the point where it could be wrong." — Author, 2026-09-21[^q4]

For this claim to stand up to testing, one must first settle what counts as 'explained'. The author has said he is not yet sure of that criterion.

## Tests proposed in the main paper <span class="st st-hyp">Hypothesis</span> { #predictions data-toc-label="Tests proposed in the main paper" }

- **Entanglement–distance correspondence**, **decoherence and the classicalization of geometry**, **a minimal model** → [Physics](../interpretations/physics.md#tests)
