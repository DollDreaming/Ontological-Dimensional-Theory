# Ontological Dimensional Theory

**A theory that interprets how existence establishes, connects and extends itself in nine stages, from the 1st to the 9th dimension.** Its Korean name is 존재론적 차원이론; in English it is the Ontological Dimensional Theory (ODT).

> An object starts from a point and, by overlapping itself, forms a new phase; that accumulated phase itself makes up the dimension of meaning.
>
> — The main paper, "Summation"[^q1]

## The author's three claims <span class="st st-hyp">Hypothesis</span> { data-toc-label="The author's three claims" }

1. **Time is change, and it is entropy.** Time is not an axis flowing outside; it is the phenomenon that arises when the structure of each dimension changes. → [Time](principles/time.md)
2. **There is nothing that the nine-dimensional fractal structure cannot explain.** Physics, life, consciousness and society are read with the same 1st–9th-dimension structure. The author himself states that this is the point where the theory could be wrong. → [Open problems](research/open-problems.md#falsification)
3. **Both the metaphysical and the physical are explained by the 1st to 9th dimensions.** But the higher one climbs from the 1st to the 9th, the greater the influence of the metaphysical. → [Core principles](principles/index.md#tiers)

## How to read this wiki

This wiki is based on the author's main paper and on what the author has said since, and each page carries only its current content. The author's words are given verbatim in quotation marks with a date (in this English edition, as translations with the Korean original in a note). Explanations of a different kind carry a label; a sentence without a label belongs to the author's theory.

| Label | Meaning |
|---|---|
| <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span> | A pairing of a concept in another field with ODT's framework |
| <span class="st st-bor">Borrowed</span> | An assumption borrowed from another theory |
| <span class="st st-hyp">Hypothesis</span> | A claim or prediction not yet tested |
| <span class="st st-rev">Open</span> | A question not yet settled |

Work on setting the formulas up as calculable formulas is in progress. → [Open problems](research/open-problems.md)
