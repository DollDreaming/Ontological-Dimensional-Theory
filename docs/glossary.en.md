# Glossary

**Terms needed to read ODT.** Each term is used in one sense only. The Korean term follows in parentheses.

Accumulation and overlap (누적, 중첩)
:   Overlap is the act of overlapping; accumulation is the build-up of that overlapping. They are two sides of the same generative process. Superposition in quantum mechanics is written as 'quantum superposition'.

Amplitude (진폭, A)
:   The minimal size of the potential influence an object has when it exists. → [1st dimension](dimensions/1-point.md)

Arrangement rule (배열 규칙, A)
:   The rule that arranges the fields of the 3rd dimension into a three-dimensional structure, not as a simple sum but by particular patterns and rules. A different symbol from the amplitude A of the 1st dimension. → [4th dimension](dimensions/4-volume.md)

Center line (중심선)
:   The trajectory in the 7th dimension that overlaps most and stands out clearly; it corresponds to the memory, history and identity of the object. → [7th dimension](dimensions/7-center.md)

Circular structure (순환 구조)
:   The structure in which the definition at the 9th dimension becomes the 1st dimension of the next interpretation. → [Linked structure](principles/linked-structure.md)

Dimension (성)
:   A stage by which existence builds up, from the 1st to the 9th. Each dimension adds one geometric axis, stacked on the axes before it. → [Core principles](principles/index.md)

Field of influence (영향의 장)
:   The range and wave-like character of the influence that gathered relations exert on their surroundings. "Field" (場) is a different word from the "tension" (張力) of the 6th dimension, though the two sound alike in Korean. → [3rd dimension](dimensions/3-plane.md)

Fractal structure (프랙탈 구조)
:   Hierarchical recursion in which every dimension holds within it an inner structure of the 1st to 9th dimensions. → [Linked structure](principles/linked-structure.md)

Integrated whole (통합체)
:   The whole in which structures on different layers overlap and integrate. 'Integration' belongs to the 5th dimension, 'synthesis' to the 9th. → [5th dimension](dimensions/5-multilayer.md)

Key to interpreting everything (만물해석키)
:   The author's name for the starting point of ODT: a key for reading everything with the same structure.

Layer (층위)
:   In the 5th dimension, the different sides (social, sensory, biological and so on) that make up one existence. → [5th dimension](dimensions/5-multilayer.md)

Level (계층)
:   Up and down within the fractal and the cycle. The 9th dimension of one level becomes the 1st dimension of a larger level. → [Linked structure](principles/linked-structure.md)

Object (객체)
:   Existences and targets; everything ODT interprets.

Phase (위상, φ)
:   The state in which an existence is aligned. The φ of the 1st dimension is its smallest form; in the 2nd dimension it decides resonance and cancellation; and the change of that alignment is time.

Reference, semantic and holistic definition (기준정의, 의미정의, 종합정의)
:   The three definitions held by the 1st, 5th and 9th dimensions. → [Core principles](principles/index.md#tiers)

Residual trace (잔흔)
:   The trajectory in the 7th dimension that overlaps less and remains faint. When speaking of the line, it is the residual line. → [7th dimension](dimensions/7-center.md)

Resonance and cancellation (공명, 상쇄)
:   The strengthening (resonance) or weakening (cancellation) of a connection according to the phase difference of two objects. → [2nd dimension](dimensions/2-line.md)

Resonance and reflection, projection (반향과 반사, 투사)
:   The re-sounding of the 7th dimension's center line and residual traces as they vibrate with a stimulus (resonance, reflection), and sending it outward (projection). The working of the 8th dimension. → [8th dimension](dimensions/8-resonance.md)

Source point (근원점, σ₀)
:   The point from which the field of influence spreads in the 3rd dimension. → [3rd dimension](dimensions/3-plane.md)

Stimulus (자극, S)
:   The influence or information from inside and outside that acts on the 7th dimension at the 8th. Its substance is energy. This theory does not separate inner and outer stimuli. → [8th dimension](dimensions/8-resonance.md)

Structural declaration (구조적 선언)
:   The starting point of the formulas: they express the phase relations and working direction of each dimension. They are now moving to a stage where they are set up as calculable formulas and checked. → [Open problems](research/open-problems.md)

Synthesis operator (종합 연산자, Ω)
:   The operation that processes, interprets and compresses the 1st to 8th dimensions to draw out the total definition of an object. → [9th dimension](dimensions/9-synthesis.md)

Tension (장력, 𝒯)
:   The internal pressure arising from asymmetry between the layer weights or states within the integrated whole of the 5th dimension; the driving force of the 6th dimension's directionality. → [6th dimension](dimensions/6-flow.md)

Tier (층)
:   A grouping of the nine dimensions: the physical tier (2nd–4th), the metaphysical tier (6th–8th) and definition (1st, 5th, 9th). → [Core principles](principles/index.md#tiers)

Time (시간)
:   Change — the change of the entropy of space. It is not an axis set beside space. The $t$ in the formulas is a parameter that counts change. → [Time](principles/time.md)

Topological accumulation (위상적 누적, phase overlap, ⨁)
:   An operation in which phases overlap and build up, rather than a simple sum. Generation between dimensions is this. → [Core principles](principles/index.md#topological-accumulation)
