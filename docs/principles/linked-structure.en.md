# Linked structure

**The definition at the 9th dimension becomes the 1st dimension of the next interpretation, and every dimension holds the 1st to 9th dimensions within itself.** This circulation and recursion is called the linked structure.

> "It's a fractal structure running from E₁ to E₉..." — Author, 2026-04-08[^q1]

## Circular structure

- The 9th and the 1st dimensions have essentially the same structure, and so they form a hierarchical circular structure.
- The final definition of an object ($E_9$) becomes a new reference point of interpretation (New $E_1$) and acts as a transition link that starts an interpretation in a larger system or from another viewpoint.

> "E₁ is the quantized form of the original of E₉." — Author, 2026-09-20[^q2]

The 9th dimension is not the 1st with time taken away. The author likens the 1st and the 9th to a tree and a forest, and adds that it does not always turn out that way.

> "The 9th isn't simply subtracting time. It's like a tree (E1) -> a forest (E9). Though it doesn't always turn out that way." — Author, 2026-09-07[^q3]

## Fractal structure

From the 1st–9th-dimension interpretation of an object, you can take one dimension (for example the 4th), set it as a new 1st dimension, and apply the 1st–9th process again. This gives a basis for checking the consistency of an interpretation by itself, and it connects the part (micro) and the whole (macro) with the same logical structure.

$$
E_n \Rightarrow \left\{ E_1^{(n)}, E_2^{(n)}, \dots, E_9^{(n)} \right\}
$$

A single existence $E_n$ defined at the nth dimension itself has an inner structure of another 1st to 9th dimension.

> "Space is fractal: at every level, the same E₁–E₉ repeat self-similarly." — Author, 2026-04-08[^q4]

> "Even within E₂ there are E₁–9. Then wouldn't there be such a fractal structure among E₃ as well?" — Author, 2026-09-21[^q5]

Words are the same. Inside a single word there are the 1st to 9th dimensions, and because they differ a little from person to person, misunderstandings arise.

> "Inside every word, E1–E9 exist. And words don't correspond one-to-one; they correspond to relatively similar words or get recombined. Look in detail and each person's own understanding of a word's E1–E9 differs. That's usually how misunderstandings arise, and it's also why, in international treaties and the like, people argue over every single character and particle of a word." — Author, 2026-09-24[^q6]

### Mathematical recursion

$$
E_n = \Omega_n \left\{ E_1^{(n)}, E_2^{(n)}, \dots, E_9^{(n)} \right\}
$$

Every dimension forms one total existence through the synthesis operator $\Omega$, yet each dimension $E_n$ also holds another $\Omega_n$ system inside. Structurally this is the self-similarity of a fractal set, logically a meta-ontology, and mathematically a recursive definition of a function.

## Philosophical implications

1. **Infinite depth of being** — No existence reaches a 'complete definition'. The 9th dimension is the last definition of one cycle, but the moment something is defined, a 1st-dimension question ("As what does it exist?") arises again within it.
2. **Identity of whole and part** (holographic ontology) — The structure of the whole repeats within its parts. This model is an interpretive framework for the whole and works the same way at a single point within it.
3. **Self-generation of meaning** (autopoietic meaning) — Meaning is not given from outside. An existence recursively extends its own structure and creates its own definition.
