# Time

**Time is change.** It is not a separate axis set beside space, but the change of structure present in every dimension — that is, the change of entropy. Where there is no change, time too has stopped.

> "Time is nothing but the change in the entropy of space. When energy (velocity, mass) changes, entropy changes, and that change is time itself." — Author, 2026-09-21[^q1]

> "If there is no change in entropy, time has stopped." — Author, 2026-09-21[^q2]

## Time and space

> "Time and space are clearly different in nature, yet connected. They are not placed on the same line." — Author, 2026-09-21[^q3]

Space is what is overlapped; time is the overlapping itself.

> "Space is the object of overlap (what is overlapped) and is reversible; time is the process of overlap (the overlapping itself) and is irreversible." — Author, 2026-04-08[^q4]

That is why time flows differently for each existence.

> "The reason time cannot help but be relative for each being is that each being's cycle has a different speed." — Author, 2026-04-08[^q5]

## From change to flow

Time is not a background against which something happens; it is the condition under which an object can change, and the flow itself. Only when the arrangement of objects changes, when the sensitivity of existence changes in amplitude, and when the phase of an object begins to shift, does time take on meaning.

> As the phase shifts → change arises,
> as change accumulates → direction forms,
> as direction persists → flow arises.
>
> — The main paper, "Interpretation of Time"[^q6]

Time is not a flow that runs through the dimensions; it is a phenomenon that arises when the structure of each dimension changes. Each object is not fixed in time but is a flowing entity that comes to exist by aligning itself. Time is the connecting link that lets things move across dimensions and an ontological parameter that leads the change, decision, intention and unfolding of existence. So the $t$ in the formulas is read not as an axis but as a parameter that counts change.

The path of time is not in one dimension alone.

> "Why is the 6th a time path? To call it simply a time path — doesn't a time path exist in every dimension?" — Author, 2026-09-07[^q7]

## The record of time

Time is recorded as information separately from structure. The author holds that its accumulation produces the 7th dimension and produces direction.

> "Yes, that's right. That's why time should be recorded separately as information, right?" — Author, 2026-09-22[^q8]

> "Time is only change. But can't the accumulation of that time produce E7? And it's just that such accumulation of time produces direction." — Author, 2026-09-24[^q9]

→ [7th Dimension · Center](../dimensions/7-center.md)

## Alignment and the speed of time <span class="st st-hyp">Hypothesis</span> { data-toc-label="Alignment and the speed of time" }

- Alignment (order ↑) → information compressed → the flow of time slows.
- Chaos (disorder ↑) → information spreads → the flow of time speeds up.

Time is not absolute; it is a relative variable that flows according to the speed at which information aligns. → [Information theory](../interpretations/information.md)

## Readings in other fields <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span> { data-toc-label="Readings in other fields" }

- **Physics** — Time is not the coordinate t but a function of phase change, $\tau = \Phi(\Delta\varphi)$, and entropy is read as the degree of phase imbalance of existence. Here $\Phi$ is the function that gives time, a different symbol from the $\Phi$ of [topological accumulation](index.md#topological-accumulation). → [Physics](../interpretations/physics.md)
- **Information theory** — Time is read as the time derivative of entropy, $dS/dt = R(t)$. Here $S$ is entropy and $R(t)$ its rate of change — different symbols from the stimulus $S$ and the reflection–projection operator $R$ of the 8th dimension. → [Information theory](../interpretations/information.md)
