# 9th Dimension · Synthesis (Holistic Definition)

**Consequence of the whole overlap → definition.**

## Definition

The final definition of an object. It synthesizes every process from the 1st to the 8th dimension (existence, relation, field, structure, integration, directionality, center line, cognition) and interprets the final entity. This definition connects with the 1st dimension to form a circular structure. The 9th dimension is the holistic definition of the nine.

## Structural declaration

$$
E_9(t) = \Omega\big(E_1(t), E_2(t), \dots, E_8(t)\big)
$$

- **$E_9(t)$** — the comprehensive, final definition of the object.
- **$E_1(t), \dots, E_8(t)$** — all the preceding dimensions that make up and define the object.
- **$\Omega(\dots)$** (synthesis and definition operator) — the final operation that processes, interprets and compresses all the information of the 1st to 8th dimensions to draw out the total essence of the object.

## Interpretation

> What is the object?

An object that began in the 1st dimension with the minimal definition of 'exists / does not exist' reaches, through eight stages, a comprehensive definition of 'what kind of entity is it?'.

## Circulation: the 1st dimension of the next interpretation

The 9th and the 1st dimensions have essentially the same structure. The final definition of an object ($E_9$) becomes a new reference point of interpretation (New $E_1$) and acts as a transition link that starts an interpretation in a larger system or from another viewpoint. → [Linked structure](../principles/linked-structure.md)

> "E₁ is the quantized form of the original of E₉." — Author, 2026-09-20[^q1]

The 9th dimension is not the 1st with time taken away.

> "The 9th isn't simply subtracting time. It's like a tree (E1) -> a forest (E9). Though it doesn't always turn out that way." — Author, 2026-09-07[^q2]

The 9th dimension is the last definition of one cycle, but the moment something is defined, the question of the 1st dimension arises again within it. So no existence reaches a complete definition.
