# 6th Dimension · Direction (Vectored Flow)

**Flow of the integrated whole → possibility.**

## Definition

The dimension in which a multilayered, integrated existence takes on directionality (a trajectory of possibility). It does not stay in a structured state; it explains where that structure can flow and which possibilities it moves along.

> "The 6th: directionality and possibility — it's probably easiest to understand as a kind of parallel universe." — Author, 2026-09-07[^q1]

## Structural declaration

$$
E_6(t) = \nabla E_5(t)
$$

- **$E_6(t)$** — the direction an existence heads toward and the trajectory of its potential possibilities.
- **$E_5(t)$** — the state of the multilayered, integrated existence that gives rise to directionality.
- **$\nabla$** (gradient operator) — computes the slope of the 5th-dimension integrated structure. This slope arises between the layers of the 5th dimension.
- **$\mathcal{T}(t)$** (ontological tension, imbalance) — the internal pressure that arises from asymmetry between the layer weights or states within the 5th dimension. This tension is the actual driving force that sets $\nabla$ to work.

## Interpretation

> Toward what directionality and possibility does the object move?

As water flows along differences in height, the integrated whole of the 5th dimension has trajectories of direction and possibility that unfold naturally along the slope of its own structure. Each such leaning is the 6th dimension.

> "What leans that way is e6.... And countless such e6 would gather to form e7." — Author, 2026-06-13[^q2]

## Features and extensions

Many paths of possibility lie open, but which path will be taken is not yet decided; this flow accumulates and is fixed in the 7th dimension as the center line and residual lines. Physically it corresponds to the direction of a vector field, socially to tendencies, and biologically to the potential paths of evolution.

Supplementary commentary: when a new spatial axis is added to the 5th dimension, it can no longer be shown as an intuitive image. So all the components and relations of the 5th dimension are synthesized and simplified to a point on a solid plane. This object draws a trajectory of change as time flows, and this new trajectory can be seen as the 'directionality (gradient)' or possibility along which the 5th-dimension object moves.

> "Add one more dimensional axis to that — then what would arise?" — Author, 2026-09-07[^q3]

The 6th dimension is not the path of time itself; a path of time exists in every dimension. → [Time](../principles/time.md) The author holds that thinking that questions and answers itself, and requests received from outside, also become directionality.

> "Generally, doesn't a request for a response, or an internal loop of thought itself, become a kind of directionality?" — Author, 2026-07-29[^q4]

These possibilities of the 6th dimension touch on chaos theory, the quantum cat-in-a-box experiment and the idea of parallel worlds.

> "E6... chaos theory... the quantum cat-in-a-box experiment... parallel-world theory" — Author, 2026-07-22[^q5]

In the reading of relativity, tension plays the same role as the energy–momentum tensor. → [Physics](../interpretations/physics.md)
