# 5th Dimension · Multilayer (Multilayer Layer System)

**Overlap of volumes → integrated whole.**

## Definition

The dimension in which the structures formed in the 4th dimension overlap and integrate across several layers. Not a single structure, but life, consciousness, society, the senses and so on combining into one multilayered system. The 5th dimension is the semantic definition of the nine.

## Structural declaration

$$
E_5(t) = \sum_k W_k(t)\, E_k(t)
$$

- **$E_5(t)$** — the state of the multilayered, integrated existence (the total entity).
- **$E_k(t)$** — the structural property of the kth layer taking part in the integration, such as the social, sensory or biological layer.
- **$W_k(t)$** (layer weight) — the relative importance or working strength of each layer; these weights decide each layer's influence within the integrated whole.
- **$\sum_k$** (integration and overlap operator) — the layers, weighted, form the whole.

## Interpretation

> By what combination is the object made up, so that it forms an integrated meaning?

It is like the five human senses, where stimuli from different layers integrate and overlap into one synaesthesia; or like one object whose social role, biological make-up and chemical reactions — structures on different layers — overlap and fuse into one total entity.

> "The 5th: it's easy to understand if you think about how humans perceive and remember their own five senses." — Author, 2026-09-07[^q1]

> "The human five senses perceive their separate dimensions (waves) as one. It is the same as how the molecular, chemical, quantum and social parts that make up a human are each their own dimension, yet make up a single human." — Author, 2026-09-07[^q2]

## Features and extensions

The 5th dimension is the dimension of systemic integration; it lets us see existence from the viewpoint of the whole, beyond a single structure. It becomes the ground on which directionality and possibility appear in the 6th dimension.

The layers of the 5th dimension are not a property of a group made by bundling many existences. They are different sides of one existence brought together as one.

> "Is the 5th really a collective property? … Isn't it seeing as one what the blind men touching the elephant were doing, just seeing different sides?" — Author, 2026-09-07[^q3]

Language is the same. When many languages and images point to one object, they are understood as one.

> "Even if I say '사과' in Korean like this, when English, Japanese, Chinese characters, and video and images point to an apple, you understand, don't you?" — Author, 2026-09-24[^q4]

The supplementary commentary gives the tesseract and cel animation as examples, and writes that, read at the 5th dimension, a human is defined as the synthesized sum of the quantum-mechanical, electrochemical, biological and cognitive-social worlds.
