# 4th Dimension · Volume (Form Volume)

**Overlap of planes → structure.**

## Definition

The dimension in which the fields of influence formed in the 3rd dimension are arranged into a three-dimensional structure. It goes beyond the spread of influence to explain how existences and relations form patterns and become concrete structures.

## Structural declaration

$$
E_4(X,t) = A\big(E_{3i}(x_i,t),\, E_{3i}(x_j,t),\, \dots\big)
$$

- **$E_4(X,t)$** — the state and manner of arrangement of the whole structure (form) at time t.
- **$E_{3i}(x_i,t)$** — the intensity and dynamics of the individual fields of influence that make up the structure; the material of the 4th dimension.
- **$X$** (set of structural coordinates) — the set of position coordinates $(x_i, x_j, \dots)$ of the 3rd-dimension fields that make up the structure; the key variable that defines the form.
- **$A(\dots)$** (arrangement and structuring operator) — the combination rule (patterning rule) by which the 3rd-dimension fields are arranged into a three-dimensional structure, not as a simple sum but according to particular patterns and rules. It is a different symbol from the amplitude $A$ of the 1st dimension.

## Interpretation

> Where and how is the object arranged?

The combination of existences makes one shape and order. It is at this stage that the concept of 'form' first arises.

## Features and extensions

The 4th dimension is the dimension of order, structure and shape, and it explains systems with structure, such as biological and physical objects. This structure is not static; it is rearranged and changes over time, and it prepares the ground on which different structures overlap in the 5th dimension.

In the reading of quantum field theory, a particle is a structured pattern of fields — that is, the 4th dimension. → [Physics](../interpretations/physics.md)
