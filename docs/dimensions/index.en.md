# The nine dimensions at a glance

**Establishment of existence → relation → influence → structure → integration → direction → center → resonance → definition.** Each dimension arises from the overlap of the dimensions before it, and the definition at the 9th dimension becomes the 1st dimension of the next interpretation.

| Dim. | Name | Overlap → result | Key question | Tier |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| [1](1-point.md) | Point (Existence Point) | Minimal establishment of an object | Does the object exist or not? | Definition (reference) |
| [2](2-line.md) | Line (Connection Line) | Continuous overlap of points → relation | How do objects relate to one another? | Physical |
| [3](3-plane.md) | Plane (Field Plane) | Overlap of lines → field of influence | What influence does the object have on its surroundings, and how far? | Physical |
| [4](4-volume.md) | Volume (Form Volume) | Overlap of planes → structure | Where and how is the object arranged? | Physical |
| [5](5-multilayer.md) | Multilayer (Multilayer Layer System) | Overlap of volumes → integrated whole | By what combination does the object form an integrated meaning? | Definition (semantic) |
| [6](6-flow.md) | Direction (Vectored Flow) | Flow of the integrated whole → possibility | Toward what direction and possibility does the object move? | Metaphysical |
| [7](7-center.md) | Center (Axis of Trace) | Accumulation of flow → trajectory and memory | What are the central trajectory and traces the object holds? | Metaphysical |
| [8](8-resonance.md) | Resonance (Resonant Feedback) | Vibration of the center → cognition and response | How does the object respond, and how does it project? | Metaphysical |
| [9](9-synthesis.md) | Synthesis (Holistic Definition) | Consequence of the whole overlap → definition | What is the object? | Definition (holistic) |

The meaning of the tiers is in [Core principles](../principles/index.md#tiers).

## How to read the formulas

The formula on each page is a structural declaration. It expresses the phase relations and working direction of that dimension. The formulas are now moving to a stage where they are set up as calculable formulas and checked; places not yet settled are in [Open problems](../research/open-problems.md). The $t$ in the formulas is not an axis but a parameter that counts change. → [Time](../principles/time.md)

## What each page contains

- **Definition** — what the dimension means
- **Structural declaration** — the formula and its variables
- **Interpretation** — the question the dimension asks
- **Features and extensions** — how it leads to the next dimension, and the author's explanations
- **Correspondences in other fields** — pairings with concepts in other fields <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span>
