# 7th Dimension · Center (Axis of Trace)

**Accumulation of flow → trajectory and memory.**

## Definition

Center and residual trace. Among the trajectories of the many possibilities unfolded in the 6th dimension, or the trajectories that were continuously chosen and remained, the part where they overlap and stand out clearly is defined as the **center line**, and the part that does not as the **residual trace**. It can also be seen as an expression of accumulated trajectories through averaging and integration.

> "The overlap of E6 generates the center line and the residual lines, and E7 is what interprets them." — Author, 2026-04-08[^q1]

## Structural declaration

The directionality of the 6th dimension is accumulated and averaged over time to obtain the central trajectory.

$$
E_7(t) = \left\langle \int_0^{t} E_6(\tau)\, d\tau \right\rangle
$$

- **$E_7(t)$** — the central trajectory and traces of the object accumulated up to time t.
- **$\int_0^t \dots\, d\tau$** (integral) — accumulating the directionality of the 6th dimension from the past to the present.
- **$\langle \dots \rangle$** (average) — averaging the accumulated trajectory to draw out the center as a common denominator, beyond momentary directionality (the 6th dimension).

This formula is one expression of the middle of the accumulated trajectories, that is, the center line. The 7th dimension holds the residual traces as well as the center line.

## Interpretation

> What are the central trajectory and traces the object holds?

Moving beyond momentary directions and possibilities, it reveals the central and outer trajectories as a common denominator accumulated with its diversity included.

The 6th dimension gathers into the 7th.

> "What leans that way is e6.... And countless such e6 would gather to form e7." — Author, 2026-06-13[^q2]

What the 7th dimension accumulates is not only the chosen directions but the whole of the possibilities and directionality of the 6th dimension. Trajectories that overlap much become the center line; those that overlap less remain as residual traces. The 7th dimension interprets the pattern that emerges and uses it as a frame and material for thought.

> "Rather than an accumulation of selection history, E7 accumulates the whole of E6's possibilities and directionality, finds patterns there and learns them (the center line and residual lines), forming a kind of frame or material for thought — and what if E8 then circles through it?" — Author, 2026-07-27[^q3]

## Features and extensions

It defines the center of an object, and through this a definition of the self becomes possible. If the 6th dimension is the map of possibility, the 7th is the path the object has walked — its memory and history. It corresponds to statistical average trajectories, the long-term stability of patterns and the memory of a system, and becomes the reference point for responding to stimuli in the 8th dimension. → [8th Dimension · Resonance](8-resonance.md)

The intuitive image of the 7th dimension is an axis that stacks, layer upon layer, the solid planes left by the 6th dimension's trajectories of change. Along this axis, the results of two or more conflicting or complementary directions accumulate to form the center line, while the directions not chosen and the trajectories that accumulated relatively little remain as residual traces.

The 7th dimension is not only the single line in the middle. The residual traces have meaning too.

> "Is the 7th merely a common point running through? Are the residual traces meaningless?" — Author, 2026-09-07[^q4]

So the 7th dimension is closer to a distribution than to a single average: it holds both the center line gathered in the middle and the residual traces spread around it.

> "E7... normal distribution. Statistics. Quantum mechanics" — Author, 2026-07-22[^q5]

The author holds that the accumulation of time produces the 7th dimension. → [Time](../principles/time.md)

In the reading of relativity, the center line corresponds to a geodesic. → [Physics](../interpretations/physics.md)
