# 3rd Dimension · Plane (Field Plane)

**Overlap of lines → field of influence.**

## Definition

The areal structure formed as lines gather — that is, the field of influence an existence exerts on its surroundings. Widening out from relation (the 2nd dimension), it explains the range of influence and the wave-like character that gathered relations form.

## Structural declaration

The influence-field function of the 3rd dimension is defined through the change over time and the spatial distribution of the 2nd dimension.

$$
E_3(x,t) = \partial_t E_2(x,t) + \nabla E_2(x,t)
$$

- **$E_3(x,t)$** — the magnitude and dynamics of the field of influence at time t and spatial coordinate x.
- **$E_2(x,t)$** — the set of individual relations (2nd dimension) that make up the field.
- **$\partial_t E_2$** (rate of change over time) — the rate at which the strength of 2nd-dimension connections changes over time; it reflects the genesis and decay of the field.
- **$\nabla E_2$** (spatial gradient) — the gradient of the spatial distribution of 2nd-dimension connections; it decides how the field propagates through space and takes on wave-like character.
- **$\sigma_0(t)$** (source point) — the point from which the field of influence spreads.
- **$\alpha(t)$** (attenuation coefficient) — how much the influence weakens with distance.

## Interpretation

> What influence does the object have on its surroundings, and how far does it reach?

The 3rd dimension explains the wave-like effect of existence, and corresponds to concepts such as electromagnetic fields, gravitational fields and social ripple effects.

## Features and extensions

The sum of relations acts as one field and influences its surroundings. It is the basis for modelling emotion, waves and flows of energy, and, going beyond propagation on a plane, it becomes the foundation of the structural arrangement of the 4th dimension.

In the reading of information theory, this field is a field of information flow, written as the information continuity equation $E_3(x,t) = \nabla\cdot I(x,t) + \partial I/\partial t$. Here $I$ is information, and the equation means 'phase change of existence = transfer of information'. → [Information theory](../interpretations/information.md)
