# 2nd Dimension · Line (Connection Line)

**Continuous overlap of points → relation.**

## Definition

The linear connection between objects — the dimension in which point and point form a relation and interact. It goes beyond simple "exists / does not exist" to explain how two existences are connected.

## Structural declaration

$$
E_2(t) =
\begin{cases}
A_i(t)\,A_j(t)\cos\big(\varphi_i(t)-\varphi_j(t)\big), & E_{1i}(t)\neq 0 \ \text{and}\ E_{1j}(t)\neq 0 \\
0, & E_{1i}(t)=0 \ \text{or}\ E_{1j}(t)=0
\end{cases}
$$

- **$E_2(t)$** — the strength of the connection between two objects i and j at time t.
- **$E_{1i}(t),\,E_{1j}(t)$** — the states of existence of the two objects taking part in the connection. A connection holds only when both objects exist.
- **$A_i(t)A_j(t)$** (interaction strength) — the maximal possibility of interaction from the combined amplitudes of the two objects; it comes from $A(t)$ of the 1st dimension.
- **$\varphi_i(t)-\varphi_j(t)$** (phase difference) — the difference between the minimal alignment states of the two objects. This difference decides resonance (reinforcement) or cancellation (weakening).

## Interpretation

> How do objects relate to one another?

The direction and strength of a relation go beyond physical connection and become a medium that carries information, meaning and action. The moment a relation is established, the interaction of the 1st dimension's minimal variables (A and φ) shows itself as the 2nd-dimension state of resonance or cancellation.

## Features and extensions

Depending on the phase difference, resonance or cancellation occurs. The alignment of this phase difference is the key condition of phase alignment that makes up time, that is, change. → [Time](../principles/time.md) As the basic unit of interaction, the 2nd dimension is the foundation of every later dimension, and it applies directly to modelling social and psychological networks of relations.
