# 1st Dimension · Point (Existence Point)

**The minimal establishment of an object — existence and non-existence.**

## Definition

Point-object, existence/non-existence, the smallest unit that moves within change (time). That is, the starting point that refers to the very fact that an object exists. The 1st dimension is the reference definition of the nine.

## Structural declaration

$$
E_1(t) =
\begin{cases}
1\cdot A(t)\, e^{i\varphi(t)}, & \text{exists} \\
0, & \text{does not exist}
\end{cases}
$$

- **$E_1(t)$** — the ontological state of the object at time t. It is ultimately settled as a discrete value, 0 (does not exist) or 1 (exists).
- **$A(t)$** (amplitude, intensity) — the minimal size of the potential influence an object has when it exists; the seed of the minimal energy and intensity that will extend into the 3rd dimension (the field of influence).
- **$e^{i\varphi(t)}$** (phase factor) — the object's initial state of alignment φ, written in the complex form of Euler's formula; the most basic condition that decides resonance or cancellation with other objects in the 2nd dimension.

Existence and non-existence are settled as one or the other; when something exists, its intensity $A$ and phase $\varphi$ make up the smallest state of that existence.

## Interpretation

> Does the object exist or not?

The 1st dimension is the starting point of every dimension. It is not simply a point placed at physical coordinates but the answer to the question "Has existence itself been established?", and the minimal condition under which the phase of existence aligns and change (time) begins.

## Features and extensions

The 1st and 9th dimensions are essentially the same structure and form the basis of the circular fractal structure. → [Linked structure](../principles/linked-structure.md)

> "E₁ is the quantized form of the original of E₉." — Author, 2026-09-20[^q1]

The 1st dimension is not bound to any one scale. It may be a quantum point or a single existence in the macroscopic world.

> "E₁ includes a macroscopic meaning, not just the quantum scale." — Author, 2026-04-08[^q2]
