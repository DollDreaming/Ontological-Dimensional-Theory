# Unifying disciplines

**Every discipline is a different window observing a different dimension of existence.** Seen this way, disagreements between disciplines are explained as 'differences of view between dimensions'. <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span>

## Disciplines by dimension

| Dim. | Disciplines | What they deal with |
|---|---|---|
| 1 Reference | Mathematical ontology, logic | The minimal definition of 'what exists' |
| 2 Relation | Network theory, physics (interaction) | Connections between existences, forces, communication |
| 3 Field of influence | Electromagnetism, theories of social influence | Fields of relations, ripple effects |
| 4 Structure | Chemistry, biology, systems theory | The generation of form, organization and pattern |
| 5 Integration | Cognitive science, living systems, information integration | Multilayered integrated wholes of existence (life) |
| 6 Directionality | Theory of evolution, economics, dynamics | Purpose, tendency, energy flow |
| 7 Center line | Psychology, theories of memory, models of the self | Identity, memory, centrality |
| 8 Resonance and cognition | Consciousness studies, neuroscience, artificial intelligence | Response, feedback, the emergence of consciousness |
| 9 Definition | Philosophy, linguistics, the humanities | Comprehensive interpretation of meaning and value |

Broadly, physics looks at the 1st–4th dimensions, the life sciences and psychology at the 5th–8th, philosophy and the arts at the 9th, and the social and information sciences at the interactions that move across the 2nd–7th.

## The unifying function

The phenomena every discipline deals with can be written as the following general function.

$$
E_n(t) = \Phi_n\big(E_{n-1}(t), \dots, E_1(t),\, S(t)\big)
$$

- $E_n(t)$ — the state of existence of the nth dimension (the dimension where the object a discipline explains lies)
- $\Phi_n$ — the generation and transition function of that dimension (the law of the discipline)
- $S(t)$ — stimulus and environmental variables (the empirical world)

Here $S(t)$ is the environment in which the object lies. Because an object of any dimension holds the 1st to 9th dimensions within itself, the stimulus enters at that object's own 8th dimension. → [8th Dimension · Resonance](../dimensions/8-resonance.md)

## Fractal integration

Each discipline divides again into the 1st to 9th dimensions within itself. Take physics alone: the establishment of a quantum state (1st) → interaction and force (2nd) → field (3rd) → structures such as atoms (4th) → integrations such as molecules and precursors of life (5th) … unfold again. Each discipline is a miniature of the whole, and the whole of knowledge widens as the parts repeat self-similarly.

- **Energy** — Not just a physical quantity but the phase tension of existence. The energy of life and consciousness can be described in the same form.
- **Information** — Quantum, biological, linguistic and conscious information differ only in how they express the rate of phase alignment; they share one structure.
