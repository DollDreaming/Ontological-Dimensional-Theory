# Interpretation in physics

**ODT recasts spacetime as a result and the phase of existence as the cause.** It does not try to replace physical theories, but to offer the topological framework of existence in which those theories operate. <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span>

## Basic view

Modern physics assumes that "energy and information interact on spacetime". The main paper redefines this as "spacetime itself is the result of the phase alignment of existence, and energy and information appear as forms of resonance between the dimensions of existence". Where physics approaches through mathematical measurement, ODT explains its foundation as a topological structure of meaning.

## Quantum mechanics and relativity

- **Quantum mechanics (1st–3rd, 5th, 8th, 9th)** — Quantum superposition is the superposition of amplitudes and phases. The intensity of interference, $I_{ij} \propto A_iA_j\cos(\varphi_i-\varphi_j)$ — interference and resonance — is the connection strength of the 2nd dimension. Measurement and decoherence belong to the 8th dimension: the interaction of the environment with the central trajectory synchronizes or erases phases and converges to probabilities. The collapse of the wave function is read as the synthesis of phase alignment (the 9th dimension).
- **Relativity (4th–7th, 9th)** — The regular pattern of fields (3rd) makes form (4th), which becomes the source of the metric. Many layers integrate (5th) to form direction (6th), and tension and imbalance play the role of the energy–momentum tensor. The accumulation of averaged direction (7th) is the geodesic.
- **Where they connect** — When the phases and superpositions of the quantum (1st–3rd) are sufficiently aligned and integrated (4th–6th), they pass into the geometry and trajectories of relativity (7th). Because time is not an external coordinate but the change of alignment itself, the two theories' concepts of time do not clash, and observation (8th) becomes not collapse but a selection of phase for definition (9th).

## Black-hole entropy

The main paper borrows the Bekenstein–Hawking formula and starts from the fact that the entropy of a black hole is proportional to the area $A$ of its event horizon ($k_B$ is Boltzmann's constant and $\ell_P$ the Planck length).

$$
S_{BH} = \frac{k_B A}{4\ell_P^{2}}
$$

Here $S$ is entropy and $A$ is area — different symbols from the stimulus $S$ of the 8th dimension and the amplitude $A$ of the 1st.

Because this entropy is the amount of information a black hole can store, the formula implies "amount of information → geometry of the event horizon (area) → gravitational structure (curvature)". Read through ODT, it is a physical instance of the extending structure in which information makes a field (3rd), the field makes geometry (4th), structures overlap in many layers (5th), and tension and curvature arise (6th).
