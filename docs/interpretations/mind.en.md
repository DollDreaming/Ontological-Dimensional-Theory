# Consciousness and self

**The self is the center where information has accumulated (7th dimension), and consciousness is the moment that center resonates with itself (8th dimension).** <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span>

## The self — the center line of the 7th dimension

The self is the line of memory of existence accumulated at the topological center (7th dimension), a sustained, averaged pattern of information.

$$
E_7(t) = \left\langle \int_0^{t} E_6(\tau)\, d\tau \right\rangle
$$

The average is one expression of the middle of the 7th dimension, that is, the center line. The 7th dimension holds the residual traces as well as the center line. → [7th Dimension · Center](../dimensions/7-center.md)

This center line is why yesterday's I, today's I and tomorrow's I feel the same. Seen through information dynamics, the self is a self-model sustained as the result of information compression, formed when the uncertainty of inner directionality ($E_6$) is made smallest by the center line ($E_7$).

$$
S_{\text{self}} = \min_t H(E_6 \mid E_7)
$$

Here $S_{\text{self}}$ is a name meaning entropy, a different symbol from the stimulus $S$ of the 8th dimension. Philosophically, the self is not a single entity but the 'inertia of a pattern'.

## Consciousness — the resonance of the 8th dimension

Consciousness is the informational self-resonance of existence, the working of the 8th dimension by which existence recognizes and adjusts its own phase state. When the phases accumulated in the 1st to 7th dimensions interact with a stimulus, the reflection–projection operator comes into play and the inner state resonates with the incoming information. Stimuli come from outside and also from thinking with oneself. → [8th Dimension · Resonance](../dimensions/8-resonance.md)

$$
E_8(t) = R(E_7, S), \qquad C(t) = I_{\text{self}}(E_7, S) = R(E_7, S)
$$

Consciousness is read as the mutual information between one's own state ($E_7$) and the environment ($S$) — the physical and informational equivalent of "existence seeing itself".

## The relation between self and consciousness

| Item | Self | Consciousness |
|---|---|---|
| Dimension | 7th (center) | 8th (resonance) |
| Essence | Accumulated pattern, memory, central trajectory | Resonance with the center, self-referential recognition |
| Function | Maintaining consistency (identity) | Recognition and adjustment (awareness, will) |
| Information dynamics | Compression, averaging | Feedback, reinterpretation |
| Temporality | Continuity from past to present | Response from present to future |
| Interaction | Reference point for consciousness | Updater of the self |

Without consciousness, the self remains a fixed lump of memory; without the self, consciousness scatters without direction. Consciousness is the 'meta-information processor' of the self, and the self is the 'information reference axis' of consciousness. Consciousness is a dynamic process and the self a static center line; the two update each other and circulate as one system.

The main paper defines the two in one line:

$$
\text{Consciousness} = R(E_7, S), \qquad \text{Self} = \langle E_6 \rangle
$$
