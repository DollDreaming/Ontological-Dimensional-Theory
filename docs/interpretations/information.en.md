# Interpretation in information theory

**In ODT, 'to exist' means that information has arisen.** The 1st to 9th dimensions are read as stages running from the creation of information → propagation → structuring → integration → feedback → meaning-making. <span class="st st-int">Correspondence</span>

The core view is "existence = the topological structuring of information". Seen through information theory, the existence of the 1st dimension means that entropy is greater than zero — that information has arisen.

## Information-dynamic time <span class="st st-hyp">Hypothesis</span> { data-toc-label="Information-dynamic time" }

Time in ODT is the change of phase alignment, that is, the degree of change. Seen through information theory, it is the time derivative of entropy.

$$
\frac{dS}{dt} = R(t)
$$

Here $S$ is entropy and $R(t)$ its rate of change — different symbols from the stimulus $S$ and the reflection–projection operator $R$ of the 8th dimension.

- Alignment (order ↑) → information compressed → the flow of time slows
- Chaos (disorder ↑) → information spreads → the flow of time speeds up

Time is not absolute; it becomes a relative variable that flows according to the speed at which information aligns. → [Time](../principles/time.md)

## The field of existence = the field of information flow

The field of the 3rd dimension is read not as a distribution of energy but as a probability distribution of the flow and transfer of information, and it becomes a conservation equation for information flow.

$$
E_3(x,t) = \nabla\cdot I(x,t) + \frac{\partial I}{\partial t}
$$

Here $I$ is information, and the equation means 'phase change of existence = transfer of information'. The field of existence becomes the topological medium in which information interacts and transforms, and in the end the flow of energy and the flow of information cannot be told apart.

## The cycle of entropy and meaning

The nine dimensions are read by their level of entropy (high in the 1st–3rd, middle in the 4th–6th, low in the 7th–9th). Seen through information dynamics, the evolution of existence is the compression of information through decreasing entropy — the process by which meaning forms. → [Core principles](../principles/index.md)

## The informational nature of the 8th dimension and the fractal

- **The 8th dimension = a self-referential system, i.e. autopoiesis** — Seen through information dynamics, the 8th dimension is a self-referential system, that is, autopoiesis. The system feeds back information about itself to update the center line (7th) and turns stimuli into meaning (9th); the main paper notes that this matches models that see consciousness as self-information processing. It is the dimension in which information about information — meta-information — forms.
- **Fractal = self-compression of information** — To unfold a structure again into the 1st to 9th dimensions means that the information within that structure repeats creation, transfer and meaning-making. It touches on models of a holographic information world.

> Quantum mechanics = micro information dynamics, relativity = macro information dynamics, and the Ontological Dimensional Theory = a topological generative structure of information that encompasses both quantum and relativity.
